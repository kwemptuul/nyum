Privacy Policy

Buyungirizi is designed to be enjoyed without creating an account or sharing personal information. This policy explains our privacy practices in clear terms.

Developer: KWEMPTUUL   •   Effective date: 7 October 2026

Your game stays on your device.

Buyungirizi works offline. The app does not require sign-up or login, does not request device permissions, and is designed not to collect or transmit personal data.

1. About Buyungirizi

Buyungirizi is an offline word-scramble game developed by KWEMPTUUL. It provides word challenges, scoring, hints, streaks and local game progress for players aged 13 and above.

2. Information we collect

KWEMPTUUL does not collect personal information through Buyungirizi. The app does not ask you to provide a name, email address, phone number, account credentials, location, contacts, photos or other personal details.

No accountNo registration or login is required.
No analytics trackingThe app is designed without user analytics or behavioral tracking.
No advertising dataThe app does not use advertising identifiers for targeted advertising.
No permission requestsGameplay does not require access to sensitive device features.

3. Local game progress

Buyungirizi may save gameplay information such as your score, solved-word count, streak and related game preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to maintain your game experience and progress.

This locally stored game information is not intentionally transmitted to KWEMPTUUL. Removing the app or clearing its application data may erase locally stored progress, depending on your device and operating-system behavior.

4. Internet connection

The core Buyungirizi game is designed to work offline and does not require an internet connection for gameplay. No online account is required to use the game.

5. Data sharing

Because Buyungirizi is designed not to collect personal data, KWEMPTUUL does not sell, rent or share personal data collected through the app with advertisers, data brokers or other third parties.

6. Device permissions

Buyungirizi is designed so that its game features do not require permission to access sensitive information or device capabilities such as your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

7. Children and young users

Buyungirizi is intended for individuals aged 13 years and above. The app is not directed to children under 13. KWEMPTUUL does not knowingly collect personal information from users through the game.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to KWEMPTUUL outside the app, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

8. Security

We minimize privacy risk by designing Buyungirizi to function without collecting personal information for normal gameplay. Players should still use the security features provided by their device and operating system.

9. External services

The core game does not require third-party accounts or online services. If a future version introduces an optional online feature or third-party service that changes how information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

10. Changes to this policy

KWEMPTUUL may update this Privacy Policy when the app, applicable requirements or our privacy practices change. A revised policy will display an updated effective date. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel where required.

11. Your choices

Since no account is required, there is no Buyungirizi account profile to delete. Game progress stored by the app can generally be removed by clearing the app's local data or uninstalling the app, subject to the controls and backup behavior of your device.
